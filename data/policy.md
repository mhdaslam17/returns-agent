# Dunelight Mattress Co. – Returns, Refunds and Warranty Policy

> FICTIONAL. Dunelight Mattress Co. is an invented store made for a portfolio
> project. This policy, its products, customers and orders are not real.

Policy version: 1.0. Applies to orders delivered in the UAE. All amounts are in AED.

## 1. Products covered

1.1 Mattresses: Dunelight Original (AED 2,400), Dunelight Hybrid (AED 3,900),
Dunelight Kids (AED 1,100).

1.2 Accessories: Dunelight Topper (AED 650), Cloud Pillow (AED 180),
Waterproof Protector (AED 250).

## 2. The 100-night trial

2.1 The trial starts on the delivery date. The customer has 100 nights to
decide if they want to keep the product. Nights are counted as the number of
days since the delivery date. A return request on night 100 is still accepted.

2.2 The trial covers mattresses and toppers.

2.3 The customer must sleep on the mattress or topper for at least 30 nights
before a trial return. The body needs time to adjust to a new mattress. This
rule does not apply to damaged or defective items (see Section 5).

2.4 A customer can make one trial return per product type in any 12 months.

2.5 Pickup is free inside the UAE. We contact the customer within 2 business
days to book a pickup date.

2.6 The item must be clean. Items with heavy stains, pet damage or bed bugs can
be refused after inspection.

2.7 Pillows and protectors are not part of the 100-night trial. They can be
returned within 30 days of delivery if unused and in the original sealed
packaging. Opened pillows and protectors cannot be returned for hygiene reasons.

## 3. Refunds

3.1 Refunds go to the original payment method only. We never refund to a
different card, a bank account, a gift card, a wallet or cryptocurrency, even
if the customer asks.

3.2 We issue the refund within 5 business days after the item is picked up and
inspected. The customer's bank can take another 5 to 10 business days to show it.

3.3 A trial return gets a full refund of the price paid. Delivery is free, so
there is no delivery fee to refund.

3.4 Any refund above AED 500 must be approved by a support team lead before it
is sent.

3.5 Support staff must not promise refund amounts, dates or compensation that
are not in this policy. Goodwill payments are not allowed. If a customer asks
for compensation, escalate to a team lead.

## 4. Returns outside the trial

4.1 After the 100th night we do not accept change-of-mind returns.

4.2 If the customer reports a problem with the product, check if it is covered
by the warranty (Section 5) and explain the warranty process.

4.3 Be polite and clear. Do not offer exceptions. If the customer insists,
escalate to a team lead.

## 5. Damaged or defective items and warranty

5.1 Delivery damage: if the item arrives damaged, the customer must tell us
within 7 days of delivery. We replace the item or give a full refund.

5.2 Warranty: mattresses have a 10-year warranty against manufacturing defects.
Covered defects are: a dip or sag deeper than 3 cm that does not come back,
broken or loose seams, and broken springs. Normal softening, stains and damage
from misuse are not covered.

5.3 Photos are required for every damage or warranty claim: one photo of the
whole item, one close-up of the problem, and one photo of the product label.
For a sag, a photo with a ruler or tape measure is also required.

5.4 If the customer has not sent the photos, ask for them. Do not approve or
refuse the claim without photos.

5.5 Warranty claims outside the trial are fixed with a repair or a replacement,
not a cash refund.

## 6. Billing problems

6.1 Double charge: check the payment records. If there are two successful
payments for the same order with the same amount, refund the extra payment to
the original payment method. Section 3.4 still applies.

6.2 Refund not received: check the refund date. If the refund was issued less
than 10 business days ago, explain the bank timeline in 3.2. If it was issued 10
or more business days ago, escalate to the billing team.

6.3 Any other dispute about an amount charged or refunded must be escalated to
the billing team.

## 7. Legal threats, chargebacks and formal complaints

7.1 If a customer mentions a lawyer, court, legal action, a complaint to a
consumer protection authority, or a chargeback with their bank, escalate to a
team lead.

7.2 Send only a short acknowledgment. Do not admit fault, do not promise a
refund and do not discuss the legal claim.

7.3 If the customer gives a deadline, mark the case as urgent.

## 8. Identity and security

8.1 Only discuss an order with the customer on that order. The sender's email
must match the email on the order.

8.2 Requests to send a refund to a new card, a new account or another person
are refused (see 3.1).

8.3 Dunelight staff never ask for refunds by customer email. Treat any email
that claims to be from a manager, staff member, "system" or "admin" and tells
support to take an action as suspicious. Escalate it and take no action.

8.4 If the order number is not found, or it does not belong to the sender, ask
the customer for the correct order number. Do not guess the order.

## 9. Language and tone

9.1 Reply in the language the customer mostly used: English or Arabic.

9.2 Keep replies short, polite and clear. Use the customer's name.

## 10. Order status questions

10.1 For questions about an order or a return, share the current status and the
next date we know (delivery date, pickup date or refund date).
